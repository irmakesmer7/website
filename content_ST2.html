Healthy Habits for Better Wellbeing

Sustainable Development Goal 3 focuses on ensuring healthy lives and promoting wellbeing for all. Although global healthcare systems and public policy are important, personal daily habits also make a big difference.

Students especially need healthy routines because academic pressure, poor sleep, stress, and irregular meals can negatively affect both physical and mental health.

Sleep and Recovery

Sleep is essential for concentration, mood regulation, and recovery. A regular sleep pattern can improve productivity and reduce stress. Students who sleep well often perform better academically and emotionally.

Exercise and Physical Health

Regular movement supports heart health, helps manage stress, and improves energy levels. Exercise does not need to be intense. Walking, stretching, and short workouts can still have meaningful benefits.

Nutrition and Hydration

Balanced meals provide energy and support the body’s daily functions. Drinking enough water also improves focus and reduces tiredness. Simple improvements, such as adding fruit and vegetables to meals, can make a noticeable difference.

Mental Wellbeing and Support

Mental health is a major part of overall wellbeing. Talking to trusted people, using breathing techniques, and asking for help when needed are all healthy actions. Support networks are important for students facing personal or academic stress.

  • Maintain a sleep routine
  • Move regularly during the week
  • Drink more water
  • Take short breaks when stressed
  • Reach out for support when necessary
